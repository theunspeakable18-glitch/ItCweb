About GitGud

GitGud is a place to get better at the games you love. Pick a game, choose the skill you want to work on, and book a one-hour session with a trainer who plays it.

Why we exist

Most players know what they want to improve but not how. Watching guides only goes so far, because nobody is there to tell you what you’re doing wrong. We pair you with a trainer who can watch you play, point out one thing at a time, and give you drills to practice on your own.

How it works

  1. Pick a game and a skill Choose Valorant, League of Legends or Counter-Strike 2, then the skill you want to build.
  2. Choose a trainer See who is available, what they focus on, and the days and hours they train.
  3. Book a time Select an open slot. You can see or cancel your sessions any time from your account.
